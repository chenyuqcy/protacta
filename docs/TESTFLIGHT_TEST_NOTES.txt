Protacta 0.1.0 (8) — What to Test

Explore the free prototype tutorials. No app account or purchase is required.

1. Paper & Craft > Classic Dart: try 3D playback, replay, view controls, step help,
   and the complete ten-action practice. Confirm your own progress separately from
   the animation. Little Fox is also available as a free 3D reference.

2. Everyday Skills: try T-shirt, towel, flat bedsheet, square-knot and tie lessons.
   Test playback, Start/Target references, help, manual confirmation, and saved
   progress after leaving and reopening a course. These are authored diagrams,
   without clothing or knot camera grading.

3. AERIS One: follow the virtual filter walkthrough. Press the highlighted release
   tab, open the rear cover, and inspect the filter movement. This is a concept
   model, rather than a manufacturer-approved manual for a real appliance.

4. Optional camera testing: use a whole flat A4 sheet for the first-fold guide.
   Check direction confirmation, hidden cues after losing the sheet, and recovery
   when it returns. Complete-course paper guidance uses the matching PTC2 revision-2
   printed sheet. Camera comparisons are experimental and do not certify folds.

5. Optional hands-free English commands require on-device speech recognition.
   Check permission handling, stopping on completion, backgrounding and recovery.

Report crashes, blocked navigation, unclear instructions and permission/recovery
issues. Premium purchases and cloud AI chat are outside this beta's testing scope.
