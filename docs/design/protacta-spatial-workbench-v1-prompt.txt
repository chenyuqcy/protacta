Use case: ui-mockup.
Asset type: a high-fidelity visual concept board for the existing native iPhone AR learning app PROTACTA. This is proposed UI design, not an actual application screenshot or evidence of implemented features.
Primary request: make the product feel like a precise futuristic spatial teaching workbench, where a learner can see the next movement directly on a 3D engine placed in the real camera view.

Composition: landscape board with two large portrait iPhone screen mockups side by side, front-on, ample breathing room, extremely narrow hardware bezels or frameless screen cards. Keep text legible and concise. Small heading outside the phones reads exactly "PROTACTA / SPATIAL LEARNING". A clearly legible footer outside the screens reads exactly "CONCEPT — PROPOSED UI". No paragraphs or explanatory callouts outside the phones.

Both screens show a plausible photorealistic phone-camera view of a clean, softly lit neutral workshop table, seen from slightly above. A tabletop-sized educational turbofan engine model is visually anchored above this table. Preserve the appearance of real materials: white outer nacelle, silver front fan and compressor, warm bronze turbine modules, dark central shaft. Show the casing and core separated enough to understand the engine structure, with coherent geometry in both screens. The real table and camera view remain visible over most of the screen. No people, hands, spaceships, cinematic characters, or unrelated props.

Design system: restrained translucent graphite/navy instrument panels, clear warm white text, very thin purposeful cyan/turquoise accents, amber reserved for the movement arrow. Palette: deep carbon #0B1C28, cool mist #EEF6F5, signal cyan #64E7E6, motion amber #E5A84F, muted slate #9BADB1. Native iOS quality typography: confident compact display lettering, highly legible sans-serif instructions, tiny monospaced step/status labels. Quiet corners, carefully aligned spacing, generous 44-point-equivalent controls. The aesthetic risk is a beautifully drawn luminous target ghost that teaches a motion, not decorative HUD clutter.

Left screen: inspection state. Slim top bar with "PROTACTA" and "AR". Small truthful status pill "Surface found". A short floating label "Low-pressure turbine" is attached by a fine leader line to the actual bronze rear turbine module. Only that selected module has a delicate cyan contour; retain its material. A compact bottom instruction strip reads "Explore the rear modules" and "STEP 4 / 6". Bottom controls are a replay icon, a microphone icon labeled "Voice", and one prominent cyan-outline button "Next step". Plenty of unobstructed model space.

Right screen: action-preview state of the same engine on the same table. The rear turbine module is partly separated along the engine's horizontal axis. A lightly translucent cyan wireframe ghost of that same module shows the intended end position slightly farther along the axis. One small amber directional arrow links the current part to that ghost. A tiny label at the target reads "Next position". This target ghost and motion arrow are the signature interaction. A short floating prompt reads "See the next move". Bottom strip again shows "STEP 4 / 6", a simple six-segment progress line, microphone icon "Voice", and "Next step". Top remains minimal.

Constraints: polished premium AR interface concept, coherent readable hierarchy, crisp native UI, subtle glow only on meaningful guidance. No fake accuracy percentages, progress statistics, temperature readouts, diagnostics, certification marks, object-recognition claims, AI-success claims, repair approval, or fabricated performance data. No dense radar circles, decorative crosshairs, illegible microtext, neon flood, purple gradients, sci-fi film references, or game HUD. The model should remain the visual hero.
