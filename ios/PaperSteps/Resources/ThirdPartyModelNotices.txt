MODEL SOURCES AND LIMITS

Helical gearmotor
Author: Innerscene Corp
Source: https://www.innerscene.com/tools/library/3d-parts/helical-gearmotor-97d45939
License: CC0 1.0 Universal
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Changes: converted GLB to USDZ, retained original mesh topology and textures, and added semantic movement groups. The source normal-map strength is preserved. This is an exterior model; an internal rotor and reduction gears are not included. Dimensions are nominal visualization geometry.

Turbofan structure
Geometry generator: jet-engine-3d by Dmitry Kulikov
Source: https://github.com/jubbon/jet-engine-3d
Source commit: 4acb4da958d34a367b11c0293235329e394baa21
Changes: exported a static structure from the procedural source, baked its 0.5-metre scale, reduced blade surface sampling, merged meshes within module/material groups, omitted invisible picking proxies and the animated flame shader, and converted the result to USDZ. The source simulator's operation, physics, audio and airflow are not included.

This geometry is an educational interpretation inspired by a CFM56-7B. It is not manufacturer CAD or an approved maintenance procedure. The authored movements illustrate relationships between virtual modules and do not prescribe real removal/installation operations.
