App uses 1K neutral-white base color, OpenGL normal, and roughness maps.
Base color is a neutral-white derivative of the lossless source scan, preserving
fiber variation through linear luminance; the source samples were blue/beige.
Normal and roughness PNG maps converted from 16-bit to 8-bit for application
size. No normal-channel inversion or roughness changes from color conversion.
Prepared 2026-11-17. See docs/FABRIC_MATERIAL_SOURCES.md in the source project.
